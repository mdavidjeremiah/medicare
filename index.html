Who it's for

Five roles, one shared queue

Every account is routed straight to its own area the moment it signs in — nobody sees a screen that isn't theirs.

Patient

Book by hospital, department and time slot, track a live token, keep a QR code on hand, and get notified when it's almost their turn.

/patient/home

Receptionist

Check patients in by QR scan or search, register walk-ins, escalate priority cases, and manage anyone who's been skipped.

/receptionist/home

Doctor

See the live queue and current patient, close out a consultation, refer a patient to another department, and review past visits.

/doctor/home

Hospital Admin

Manage departments and doctors, create staff accounts, and set the hospital's skip/rejoin policy.

/admin/home

Super Admin

Onboard new hospitals onto the platform and create the hospital admin accounts that run them.

/super-admin/home
For patients

Watch the queue move, from anywhere in the waiting room — or outside it

No more asking the receptionist "how many people are ahead of me?" The queue position updates live as each token is called.

  • Live positionStreamed straight from Supabase Realtime — no manual refresh.
  • One QR tokenGenerated at booking, scanned at check-in — nothing to fill out twice.
  • Timely nudgesA push notification when a patient is next, so they're not glued to the screen.

For front-desk & clinical staff

Check-in, walk-ins and hand-offs, without touching a spreadsheet

Receptionists scan a token or search by name; doctors see exactly who's next and can refer a patient to another department in two taps.

  • Walk-in bookingRegisters a patient and drops them straight into the live queue.
  • ReferralsA doctor can send a patient to another department without a new booking.
  • Skip & rejoin policyConfigurable per hospital, enforced by the same rules for every receptionist.
