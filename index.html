Smart booking

Fair slots without the doctor lottery.

Booking a slot reserves a place, not a specific doctor. A clinician is assigned in real time when the patient is called — so care stays fair and capacity stays balanced.

  • Pick a hospital, department, date and time in seconds
  • Half-hour booking windows across the working day
  • Real-time doctor assignment on check-in
